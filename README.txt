AI BATTLE ROYALE: PORTABLE REPORT

1. Download the entire AI-Battle-Royale-Gallery folder.
2. If it arrived as a ZIP archive, extract it completely first.
3. Open index.html in Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari.

No server, installation or internet connection is required.
Keep the HTML pages together with the app, videos, images and blender folders.
You may rename or move the outer folder. All links are relative.

Tabs: Setup, Brief, Stage 1 (3D assets), Stage 2 (Map), Stage 3 (Gameplay), Verdict, Cost & time.
The Blender projects are available through the Download .blend links in Stage 1.
