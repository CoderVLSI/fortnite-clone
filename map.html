AI Battle Royale · stage 2, map

October 2026 · Claude Opus 5.5, GPT-6.1 Sol, Gemini 4 Argon, Muse Wonder · Blender 5.1 and Unity 6

One top-down reference map, four islands
400 × 400 m

Each model built the island inside a 600 x 600 m ocean area, from its own stage 1 assets at their real sizes, with procedural terrain and water. Zones, coastline, lake, river, roads, bridges and towers come from the map.

What the brief asked for

North
Pine forest of at least 150 pines, the lake, rock clusters.
North-east
Farm with crop fields, fence lines, one farmhouse.
East
Beach with at least 30 palms.
Center
Village of 6 to 8 houses around a plaza, chests inside.
South-west
Industrial yard with 3 warehouses and a small fort from the modular kit.
West
Oak forest of at least 120 oaks with clearings.
Hills
3 lookout towers: the south cliff point and two hilltops.
Game ready
Colliders, 30 spawn points, loot across the island, a target of 60+ FPS at 1920x1080.
