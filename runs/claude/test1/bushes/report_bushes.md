# bushes

- Preview: `runs/claude/test1/bushes/preview_bushes.png` (front, 3/4, back, top)
- Objects: `bush_plain` (2,344 triangles), `bush_berry` (3,100 triangles); budget 4,000 per bush
- Size: each about 1.4 m wide and 1.1 m tall, origin at the bottom centre
- Materials: `mat_leaf`, `mat_leaf_light`, `mat_leaf_core`, `mat_berry`; leaf materials are double-sided
- Self-correction loops: 1 (leaves were thin and spiky; made them rounder and flatter-lying with larger cores so the clumps read as domes)

Weakest: the leaves are single flat cards, so from the top the clumps look tiled rather than soft and layered.
