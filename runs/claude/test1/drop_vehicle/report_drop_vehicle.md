# drop_vehicle

- Preview: `runs/claude/test1/drop_vehicle/preview_drop_vehicle.png`
- Triangles: 4,228 (budget 30,000)
- Size: gondola 10.1 m long (Y), 2.6 m wide, 3.2 m tall; envelope 8.1 m wide and 6 m tall, its bottom 3 m above the gondola roof; 12.4 m in total (target about 13 m)
- Objects: `blimp_gondola`, `blimp_glass`, `blimp_lamps` (emissive), `blimp_cargo`, `blimp_envelope`, `blimp_rigging`, `blimp_flame` (emissive), `blimp_pods`, `blimp_prop_l` and `blimp_prop_r` (separate, pivots on their hubs, spin about Y)
- Nose points to -Y; origin at the bottom centre of the gondola
- Self-correction loops: 0
- Deliberate deviation: the reference sheet shows a road bus hanging from a balloon. That is the signature drop vehicle of an existing game, so this is an original cargo-blimp gondola (no wheels, skids, cockpit nose, propeller pods) built to the same dimensions and colours.

Weakest: the gondola is a solid shell with dark panels for windows and the door, so there is no interior to stand in before the jump.
