# warehouse

- Preview: `runs/claude/test1/warehouse/preview_warehouse.png` (front, side, 3/4, back)
- Triangles: 5,332 (budget 20,000)
- Size: 14 m wide (X), 10 m deep (Y), 5 m walls, ridge at about 7.2 m; 7.7 m to the top of the vents
- Objects: `warehouse_shell` (hollow walls and floor), `warehouse_frame`, `warehouse_roof`, `warehouse_roller_door` (separate, on the +X gable, slides up), `warehouse_door` (separate, on the front wall, pivot on its hinge edge), `warehouse_crates`
- Materials: blue sheet (two tones), orange frame, roller grey (two tones), concrete, vent, wood (two tones)
- Self-correction loops: 0
- Note: the brief gives 14 m as the X width and asks the front to face -Y, so the long wall with the side door faces -Y and the roller door is on the +X gable end.

Weakest: the corrugation is two alternating blue tones on a flat wall, not real ridges, so it has no depth in grazing light.
