# Stage 2: island level

Built in Godot 4.5 instead of Unity, at the user's request. Everything in the level is a stage 1 asset or is generated by `island/scripts/build_island.gd` (terrain, water, roads, bridges, crop rows). No downloaded content.

- Project: `runs/claude/test2/island/` (open `project.godot`; main scene `scenes/island.tscn`)
- Rebuild from zero: `GODOT=/path/to/godot runs/claude/test2/rebuild.sh`
- Captures: `captures/01_topdown.png` to `12_blimp.png`, `captures/flythrough.mp4` (22 s)
- Machine-written results: `island/scenes/island_counts.json`, `captures/validation.json`, `captures/performance.json`

## What was built

| Brief item | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Island about 400 x 400 m in a 600 x 600 m ocean | About 380 m across; terrain mesh covers 600 x 600 m; ocean plane extends past it |
| Scripted heightmap | 301 x 301 height grid at 2 m, 180,000 triangles: hills in the north, lake basin, flat village, farm and yard, east beach, rocky south point, three tower hills, river channel from the lake to the south coast |
| Terrain layers made in code | Grass, sand, dirt road, rock, paved yard and crop colours painted per vertex, with a generated noise texture (`textures/terrain_detail.png`) for grain |
| Water | Ocean at sea level, lake surface, river ribbon falling from the lake to the sea, two wooden bridges |
| North | 175 pines, the lake, rock clusters including both moss rocks |
| North-east | Farmhouse, three fenced crop fields (1,232 instanced crop plants, 175 fence segments), barrels and crates |
| East | Beach with 36 palms |
| Centre | 7 houses around a plaza, 5 treasure chests inside houses |
| South-west | 3 warehouses, about 40 crates, 8 ammo boxes, paved yard, fort of 16 modular-kit pieces (walls, floors, ramp, roofs) |
| West | 132 oaks with three clearings (46 more oaks scattered elsewhere) |
| Towers | 3 lookout towers: south point, south-east hill, north-west hill |
| Everywhere | 220 bushes, about 180 rocks, 14 chests, 18 ammo boxes, 12 medkits, 12 shield potions, 4 of each weapon and of grenades, one landed supply drop, cargo blimp 80 m above the ground |
| Spawn points | `Spawn_01` to `Spawn_30`, at least 52 m apart |
| Hierarchy | Grouped by zone (`Zone_Center_Village`, `Zone_North_PineForest`, ...), plus `Terrain`, `Water`, `Bridges`, `Towers`, `Scatter`, `Loot`, `Sky`, `Spawns` |
| Instancing | Trees, bushes and crops are MultiMesh instances (1,841 objects); meshes and collision shapes are shared resources |

## Game readiness (checked by `scripts/validate.gd`)

- Terrain has a height-field collider on the same grid as the mesh.
- 558 static bodies with 944 collision shapes: exact colliders on houses, warehouses, towers and kit pieces; convex hulls on rocks and props; cylinders on tree trunks.
- All 30 spawns: ground directly beneath, above water, and a player-sized capsule overlaps nothing.
- A ray dropped inside a house lands on its floor; a ray fired at a house from outside is stopped by the wall. Doors are separate nodes on their hinge pivots.

## Performance

Not measurable against the 60 FPS target here. This machine has no GPU, so Godot rendered with a CPU software rasteriser (llvmpipe). Method: 658 frames of the flythrough at 1280 x 720 while recording the movie, wall-clock time per frame.

- Average 963 ms per frame (about 1 FPS), worst 1,467 ms, in software rendering with movie encoding on.
- 3,527 draw calls and 5.2 million primitives per frame, shadows included. That is the number to watch on real hardware: it is high because every prop, loot item and house part is its own draw.

## Self-correction

1. Forests, bushes and crops were missing: a headless build cannot store MultiMesh buffers. The transforms are now kept on each node and the MultiMesh is built on load (`scripts/instanced.gd`).
2. Colours were oversaturated: this renderer reads the linear glTF vertex colours as sRGB, so the builder converts them once. Lighting and fog were also too strong.
3. Hills were too flat for cliffs and high ground, a forest tint had a hard edge, and the river stopped short of the sea.

## Weakest

- The south and west coasts are smooth rocky slopes, not the sheer cliffs of the map.
- Draw-call count is high; static props are not merged or batched.
- The paved yard and roads have jagged edges from the 2 m vertex-colour grid.
- The village plaza is bare, and the lake and river surfaces overlap visibly where they meet.
- Water has no shader (flat translucent surfaces), and only the ocean has a water volume for swimming.
